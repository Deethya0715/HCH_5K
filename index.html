Race day countdown Saturday, March 27, 2027

Why we run

A cancer diagnosis happens to the whole family. So does healing.

Hope. Cope. Heal. is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit providing mental health counseling and therapy services to pediatric cancer patients, their siblings, and their caregivers. Treatment protocols care for the body — we make sure someone is caring for the mind.

Every dollar and every gift card raised at this event is directed to one purpose: building and furnishing our specialized pediatric therapy playroom and clinical suite, a space designed so a child can put down the weight of treatment and simply be a kid.

Race day timeline

Schedule of Events

Tap any block for details. All times are Central and the festival lawn stays open the entire day — you do not need a race bib to join us.

7:30 AM Check-in, Bib Pickup & Festival Lawn Opens
Packet pickup opens at the Pavilion. Bring a photo ID (or a parent for youth runners). Day-of registration is available at the same tent while spots last. Restrooms, water, and the gear-check tent open at the same time.
9:00 AM 10K Race Kickoff
Start at the Starting Chute. Corrals open at 8:45 AM — please self-seed by expected pace. Two aid stations on course with water and electrolytes.
9:30 AM 5K Race Kickoff
Runners and walkers share the same start. Walkers and stroller teams line up at the back of the chute. The 5K route passes the full length of the Honoring Our Heroes tribute trail.
11:00 AM Food Trucks & Touch-a-Truck Open
Rotating local food trucks set up along the Food Truck Rally lane. Touch-a-Truck brings fire engines, police cruisers, and city service vehicles to the north lot — a sensory-friendly, horn-free hour runs from 11:00 to 11:30 AM.
11:30 AM Kids' 1-Mile Fun Run & Stroller/Family Care Walk
Untimed and joyful. Kids ages 4–12 run a single loop of the inner trail with volunteer course marshals at every turn. Families, strollers, wagons, and caregivers step off right behind them.
12:15 PM Junior Firefighter & Police Officer Badge Ceremony
Frisco first responders sworn in our honorary junior firefighters and officers at the Pavilion stage. Patients and siblings receive a badge, a helmet, and a very loud round of applause.
1:00 PM Family Workshop, Games Zone & Festival Activities
Running until 3:00 PM. The Family Workshop & Games Zone offers pipe cleaner crafts, ring toss, cornhole, and face painting — $3 per activity ticket, with cash, Zelle, and Venmo accepted. Community Charity Bingo follows, with prizes donated by local partners. A Kendra Scott jewelry silent auction benefits the therapy playroom suite.
3:00 PM Sponsor Recognition & Top Finisher Awards Ceremony
Awards for the overall and masters winners plus the top three finishers in each age group for the 5K and 10K, followed by recognition of the sponsors who made the therapy suite possible.
4:30 PM Event Conclusion
Lost-and-found and unclaimed packets move to the Pavilion tent until 4:30 PM, then to our office. Tribute signs may be collected after 3:30 PM or we will mail them to your family at no cost.

The tribute trail

Honoring Our Heroes Commemorative Walk

The half-mile stretch of trail between the Pavilion and the north bend is lined with tribute signs. Dedications honor childhood cancer patients, survivors, and angels. Every runner on the 5K, 10K, and family walk passes them.

Each sign carries a child's name, photo, and a short message written by their family. Gold ribbons mark those still in treatment, teal marks survivors in remission, and white doves mark the children we walk in memory of. It is the quietest part of the course, and for most people, the part they remember.

In Treatment
Gold ribbon signs for children currently fighting. Families may add a QR code linking to a CaringBridge page or meal train.
Survivor
Teal signs celebrating remission, with the date of the last treatment and a milestone the family wants the world to know about.
In Loving Memory
White dove signs placed at the shaded bend, where we hold a moment of silence at 11:25 AM before the family walk steps off.

The ask

Therapy Room Wishlist & Gift Card Drive

We are not raising money for a general fund. We are furnishing one specific room — a pediatric therapy playroom and clinical suite — and gift cards let our child life specialists buy exactly what a child in front of them needs, in the right size, the right texture, and the right week.
